# Privacy Policy — Calm Haven Uploader

Calm Haven Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel
"Calm Haven Sounds" to upload its own videos through the YouTube Data API.

- It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, to upload videos, thumbnails and playlists.
- It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data of anyone else.
- The access token is stored as an encrypted secret and can be revoked at any time at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  and the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
